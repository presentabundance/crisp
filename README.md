# Crisp's Big Day

A side-scrolling game about raising Crisp, a white cavapoo puppy with black spots on his ears, around his eyes and on his back, and a black ring at the base of his tail.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

## How to play

- **Arrows / A, D**: walk · **Space / Up**: jump · **C**: give a command, or mark "Yes!" during lessons · **X**: bark · **P**: pause · **M**: sound
- On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

## Puppy care in the game

- **Awake clock**: keep Crisp's awake time under 60 minutes by reaching a dog bed. Once he's past 40 minutes he gets sleepy and starts nipping. At 60 he has a meltdown.
- **Chew toys**: collect them so a tired nip goes onto a toy instead of a hand, a slipper or the furniture.
- **Potty**: take him to the pad or the grass spot before the meter fills. He needs to go after naps and after drinking.
- **Water**: a thirsty puppy slows down, so stop at the water bowls.
- **Socializing**: greet people and other puppies while he's rested.
- **Dangerous food**: use "Leave it" before he eats socks, wild mushrooms, chocolate or grapes.

## Story

- **Week 8, Coming Home**: learn NAME and SIT (sit opens the pet gates)
- **Week 12, The Backyard**: learn DOWN (crawl through tunnels) and LEAVE IT
- **Week 16, Big Day at the Park**: learn STAY (crosswalks) and COME (squirrel recalls), then graduate from puppy class

Lessons use timing: press C as the dot reaches the green zone to mark the moment Crisp does it right. The green zone shrinks when he's tired.
