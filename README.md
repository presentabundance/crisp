# Crisp's Big Day

A side-scrolling game about raising Crisp, a white cavapoo puppy with black spots on his ears, around his eyes and on his back, and a black ring at the base of his tail.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

## One puppyhood, no do-overs

There are no lives and no game over. The game saves automatically as you play, so quitting doesn't undo anything. You play six days, from 8 to 16 weeks old, and each day ends at bedtime however it went. Mistakes leave a mark:

- **Overtired meltdowns:** if he's awake for 60 minutes, he crashes on the floor. That isn't real rest, and his biting habit gets worse.
- **Nipping:** every nip that isn't redirected to a chew toy builds a biting habit, so he gets bitey sooner after that. Redirects slowly undo it.
- **Accidents:** when he has more accidents than good potties, he needs to go more often.
- **Eating something toxic** (wild mushroom, chocolate, grapes) means a vet visit. The day ends right there and he's slower the next day. A sock means an upset tummy.
- **Vacuum and pond scares** stay with him as fears.
- **Socializing:** greeting people and dogs while he's rested shapes how he turns out. Fewer than 4 hellos and he grows up nervous around strangers.

The ending shows Crisp at one year old and is built from everything above.

## Training that works like real training

Each command is taught the way trainers teach it, and each one gets a reliability score. Practice raises it. Rushing, repeating cues and training a tired puppy lower it. Out in the world, the chance he'll obey depends on that score and on how tired he is.

- **Name:** say it once, while he's only lightly sniffing. Then mark the moment he looks at you. Repeating his name teaches him to tune it out.
- **Sit / Down:** move the treat in short bursts so his nose stays on it. Mark the moment his bottom (or elbows) touches the floor.
- **Leave it:** keep your fist closed and mark the moment he backs off. Marking while he paws at your hand rewards pawing.
- **Stay:** step back and wait, then go back to him before you mark. Watch for wiggles.
- **Come:** call once, then run away or crouch and clap. Walking toward him turns it into keep-away.

Treats you collect pay for rewards. Without them, he only gets praise and learns more slowly.

## Puppy album

Milestones are saved as snapshots in a puppy album, the bad days along with the good ones.

## Controls

**Arrows / A, D**: walk · **Space**: jump, or hold to move the treat in lessons · **C**: give a command or mark "Yes!" · **X**: bark · **P**: pause · **M**: sound. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
